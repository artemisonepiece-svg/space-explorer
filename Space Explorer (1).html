Extreme gravity

Where gravity wins

A black hole is a region where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can leave. Matter spiraling in forms a glowing disk that can outshine whole galaxies.

Anatomy, from the outside in

Distances use rs, the Schwarzschild radius, for a black hole that does not spin.

  • 3 rs
    Innermost stable orbitThe closest circular orbit matter can hold. Inside it, gas plunges in. The bright disk ends near here.
  • 1.5 rs
    Photon sphereLight can circle the hole here. It makes the thin bright ring in the picture.
  • 1 rs
    Event horizonThe point of no return. Escaping would take a speed greater than light.
  • 0
    SingularityThe center, where general relativity predicts unlimited density. What really happens there is unknown.

How big is the horizon?

The event horizon radius grows in direct proportion to mass: about 2.95 km for each Sun's worth.
